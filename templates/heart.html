Welcome! 💓

Your heart is an amazing muscle that never stops working. It pumps blood throughout your entire body, delivering oxygen and nutrients to every cell!

Fun Facts:

• Beats 100,000 times per day
• Pumps 2,000 gallons of blood daily
• About the size of your fist
• Has 4 chambers that work together
